# Data Breach Response Plan (US focus)

## 1. Purpose

This plan sets out actions to take when a security incident involving personal information is suspected or confirmed, taking into account applicable US state breach notification laws.

## 2. Definition

A security breach is generally the unauthorised acquisition of computerised data that compromises the security, confidentiality or integrity of personal information.

Definitions and triggers vary by state.

## 3. Immediate actions

1. Contain the incident.
2. Preserve evidence.
3. Notify internal incident response lead and legal / privacy counsel.
4. Begin assessment of the nature and scope of affected information and individuals.

## 4. Notification obligations

Most US states require notification to affected residents **without unreasonable delay** when personal information has been acquired (or is reasonably believed to have been acquired) by an unauthorised person.

Many states also require notification to the state Attorney General and, in some cases, to credit reporting agencies when a threshold number of residents is affected.

Timelines and content requirements differ by state — map the affected individuals by state of residence and apply the relevant rules.

## 5. Content of notification (typical elements)

- Description of the incident
- Types of information involved
- Steps taken
- Recommendations for the individual (e.g. credit monitoring, fraud alerts)
- Contact information for questions

## 6. Documentation

Maintain a record of the incident, investigation, risk assessment and notifications made.
