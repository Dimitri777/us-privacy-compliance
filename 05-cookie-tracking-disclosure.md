## 1. Technologies we use

We and our service providers use cookies, pixel tags, local storage, SDKs and similar tracking technologies (“cookies”) on our websites and apps.

## 2. Categories and purposes

| Category | Purpose | Examples |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Strictly necessary | Site operation, security, consent management | Session cookies, load-balancing |
| Performance / Analytics | Understand usage and improve the service | Google Analytics, similar tools |
| Functional | Remember preferences and settings | Language, region |
| Advertising / Targeting | Deliver relevant ads, measure campaigns, build audiences | Ad network pixels, social plugins |

## 3. Your choices

- You can manage cookie preferences via our cookie banner / preference centre.
- You can also control cookies through your browser settings.
- Where required by law we treat Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out of sale/sharing.
- For targeted advertising opt-out see also the “Do Not Sell or Share” link and industry tools (e.g. NAI, DAA).

## 4. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal. Our practices with respect to DNT are described in the Privacy Policy. Note that GPC is treated as an opt-out signal where required.
