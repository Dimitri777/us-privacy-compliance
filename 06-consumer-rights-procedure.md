# Consumer Rights Request Procedure (CCPA/CPRA & similar state laws)

## 1. Scope

Procedure for handling consumer requests to:
- Know / Access
- Delete
- Correct
- Opt-out of sale / sharing / targeted advertising
- Limit use of sensitive personal information

## 2. Submission channels

- Webform: [URL]
- Email: [privacy@example.com]
- Toll-free number (if required): [number]
- “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link

## 3. Verification

Reasonable methods must be used to verify the identity of the consumer (and authority of any agent). The degree of verification should match the sensitivity of the request.

## 4. Response timelines

- Generally respond within **45 calendar days** of receipt.
- One extension of an additional 45 days is permitted when reasonably necessary; the consumer must be notified within the first 45 days.

## 5. Steps

1. Log the request and date received.
2. Verify identity / authority.
3. Determine the scope of the request and locate relevant personal information.
4. Apply any exceptions permitted by law.
5. Fulfil the request or provide a reasoned refusal.
6. Notify the consumer of the outcome.
7. Retain records of the request and response as required.

## 6. Opt-out of sale / sharing

Opt-out requests must be honoured as soon as feasibly possible and at the latest within 15 business days. Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals must be treated as valid opt-out requests where required by law.
