## Key US frameworks covered
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by CPRA
- Other comprehensive state laws (Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, etc.) – many concepts overlap
- Common federal considerations (sectoral laws may impose additional requirements)

> **Disclaimer:** Templates are educational starting points only and do not constitute legal advice. US privacy law is a patchwork; requirements vary by state, sector and business model. Obtain qualified legal advice for your specific situation.
