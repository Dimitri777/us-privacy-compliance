# Reasonable Security Measures  
Summary (US privacy & state data security laws)

**Last reviewed:** [Date]

## 1. Overview

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, destruction, use, modification or disclosure. The measures take into account the nature of the information, the risks involved and the resources available.

## 2. Administrative safeguards
- Written information security program / policies
- Designation of personnel responsible for security
- Employee background checks where appropriate
- Security awareness training
- Vendor due diligence and contractual security requirements
- Incident response plan

## 3. Technical safeguards
- Access controls and least-privilege principles
- Multi-factor authentication for remote and privileged access
- Encryption of personal information in transit (TLS) and at rest where appropriate
- Anti-malware, endpoint protection and vulnerability management
- Network segmentation and firewalls
- Logging and monitoring of systems that process personal information
- Secure software development practices
- Regular backups and tested recovery procedures

## 4. Physical safeguards
- Controlled access to facilities and equipment
- Secure disposal of paper and electronic media
- Visitor management

## 5. Assessment and improvement
- Periodic risk assessments
- Vulnerability scanning and, where appropriate, penetration testing
- Review of access rights
- Post-incident reviews and continuous improvement

These measures are intended to meet the “reasonable security” standard under applicable state laws (including California Civil Code § 1798.81.5 and similar statutes). Specific requirements may vary by industry and jurisdiction.
