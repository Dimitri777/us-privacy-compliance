# Service Provider / Contractor Agreement  
(CCPA/CPRA – key contractual terms)

**Template – adapt and negotiate as needed**

This Agreement is entered into between:

**Business:** [Name, address]  
**Service Provider / Contractor:** [Name, address]

## 1. Role of the parties

The Service Provider processes personal information on behalf of the Business solely for the business purposes specified in the agreement / statement of work and not for any other commercial purpose.

## 2. Restrictions on use and disclosure (CCPA/CPRA)

The Service Provider agrees that it shall **not**:
- sell or share the personal information;
- retain, use or disclose the personal information for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the contract, including for its own commercial purposes;
- retain, use or disclose the personal information outside the direct business relationship with the Business;
- combine the personal information with personal information it receives from other sources (except as permitted for limited internal use under CPRA).

## 3. Assistance with consumer rights

The Service Provider shall assist the Business in responding to consumer rights requests (access, deletion, correction, opt-out, limitation of sensitive PI) by appropriate technical and organisational measures.

## 4. Security

The Service Provider shall implement reasonable security measures appropriate to the nature of the personal information.

## 5. Subcontractors

The Service Provider shall not engage subcontractors to process personal information without prior written authorisation and without flowing down equivalent obligations.

## 6. Certification / audit

Upon request the Service Provider shall make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and shall allow reasonable audits or assessments by the Business or its designated auditor.

## 7. Return or deletion

Upon termination of the services, or upon request, the Service Provider shall delete or return personal information as instructed by the Business, unless retention is required by law.

## 8. Breach notification

The Service Provider shall notify the Business without unreasonable delay after becoming aware of a security breach affecting personal information.
