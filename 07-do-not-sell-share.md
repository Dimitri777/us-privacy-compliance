# Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information  
Opt-out mechanism (CCPA/CPRA & similar laws)

## 1. Your right

California residents (and residents of certain other states) have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of their personal information, and of the use of personal information for targeted / cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 2. How to opt out

You may exercise your right by any of the following methods:

1. Click the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link in the footer of our website.
2. Submit a request via our webform: [URL]
3. Email us at: [privacy@example.com]
4. Call us at: [toll-free number if required]
5. Enable a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser – we will treat it as a valid opt-out request where required by law.

## 3. What happens after you opt out

- We will stop selling or sharing your personal information and stop using it for cross-context behavioural advertising (subject to legal exceptions).
- We will process your request as soon as feasibly possible and within the timelines required by applicable law (generally no later than 15 business days for opt-out).
- You may be asked to provide additional information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or to process the request.
- Opting out is non-discriminatory – we will not deny goods or services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised this right (subject to limited exceptions).

## 4. Opt-out preference signals

We honour browser-based opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) in accordance with applicable law.

## 5. Authorized agents

You may designate an authorized agent to submit an opt-out request on your behalf. We may require proof of authorization and verification of your identity.
